01 / WHO WE ARE

Technology should open doors, not create barriers.

MIKROMATE LINK AFRICA is a Kenya-based business focused on digital innovation and opportunity-led solutions.

Our ambition is to make useful digital services more accessible, strengthen connections between people and organisations, and support a more inclusive digital economy across Africa.

02 / OUR PLATFORM

Meet MikroMate.

A digital platform being developed to connect people who need tasks done with people ready to do them.

Our vision is to make it easier to discover opportunities, post tasks, find suitable help and build trusted working connections — starting in Kenya, with a wider African and international outlook.
